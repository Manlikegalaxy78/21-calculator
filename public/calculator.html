01 Playing basic strategy correctly cuts the house edge to roughly 0.5%, down from 2%+ on average intuitive play. It narrows your losses; it doesn't flip the game in your favour.
02 A natural blackjack lands on about 4.8% of hands and typically pays 3:2, though some tables only pay 6:5, which is worth checking before you sit down.
03 Insurance is a bad bet regardless of your hand: its true house edge is around 7%, far worse than the base game.
04 These figures shift with table rules (soft-17, payout ratio, deck count), so this app's Table Rules panel lets your live readings match the exact table you're at.
